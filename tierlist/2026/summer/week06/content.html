Notes & Misc.

  • The Ghost in the Shell (Koukaku Kidoutai: THE GHOST IN THE SHELL) — I watched around three episodes and genuinely enjoyed this more comedic, original-manga-inspired take on a series I’ve always loved. However, it doesn’t quite fit the format of the Weekly Rankings, so I’ve decided to leave it out for now. I fully intend to come back to it and start watching again properly.

  • Chainsmoker Cat (Yani Neko) — This is an entirely personal issue rather than a judgment on the quality of the anime itself, but I simply couldn’t handle some of the hygiene-related elements in the first episode. I’m sorry, Yani Neko—I had to drop it.

  • Grand Blue Dreaming Season 3 (Grand Blue Season 3) — I was a huge fan of Season 1, but for reasons I still can’t quite explain, the series stopped working for me from Season 2 onward. I gave Season 3 another shot and made it through two episodes, but unfortunately I still couldn’t get back into it, so I dropped it.
